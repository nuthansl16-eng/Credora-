# Refund Policy (DRAFT TEMPLATE — REQUIRES LAWYER REVIEW)

## General rule
Purchases of digital points are generally final once points have been
credited to a community's leaderboard, because points have already
served their purpose (affecting a permanent, public leaderboard) at
that moment.

## When we do issue a reversal
- Duplicate or erroneous charges, verified against our payment ledger
- Payments later reversed by the payment provider (chargebacks) —
  points are reversed to keep the leaderboard accurate
- Fraud, verified through our internal review process
- Legal requirement in your jurisdiction

## What a reversal does
A reversal removes the corresponding points from the leaderboard via an
immutable `REFUND_REVERSAL` ledger entry — it does not silently edit
history. See our Leaderboard Integrity Policy.
