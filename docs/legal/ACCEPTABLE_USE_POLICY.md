You may not use this platform to:
- Create or select religion/community entries that promote hatred,
  violence, or discrimination against any group
- Impersonate another person or organization
- Attempt to manipulate the leaderboard through fraud, chargebacks
  used in bad faith, or exploiting technical vulnerabilities
- Harass, threaten, or attempt to reveal another user's private
  information (including inferred religious affiliation)
- Use automated means to create accounts or purchases
- Attempt to circumvent the non-transferability of points (e.g.
  account-sharing schemes to move points between users)

Violations may result in suspension, account termination, and — where
points were obtained fraudulently — reversal via the point ledger.
