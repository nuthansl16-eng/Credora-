# Charity & Transparency Policy (DRAFT TEMPLATE — REQUIRES LAWYER/ACCOUNTANT REVIEW)

A portion of eligible platform revenue is allocated to charitable
initiatives. Details are provided below and updated each reporting
period.

**Current disclosed allocation:** [X]% of eligible platform revenue is
allocated to charitable initiatives. ("Eligible platform revenue" means
[define precisely with your accountant — e.g. net revenue after
payment-processing fees and refunds, for a defined period]. Do not
publish a percentage claim your books cannot support.)

**Important:** an individual purchase is not itself a direct donation
unless our accounting structure specifically earmarks that transaction
to a charity in real time. Our current structure allocates a percentage
of aggregate eligible revenue over a reporting period, not per-purchase.
Do not describe a purchase as "your donation" in any UI copy unless
this changes and counsel confirms it's accurate.

## Reporting
Each allocation is recorded with: reporting period, eligible revenue,
currency, allocation percentage, allocated amount, recipient
organization, allocation date, and a receipt reference where available.
The (admin-managed) charity allocation records are the authoritative
history — the public page lists only entries with status "allocated" or
"paid," never fabricated or projected figures.
