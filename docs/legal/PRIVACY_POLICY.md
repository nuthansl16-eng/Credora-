## Sensitive data notice

Your religion/community selection is treated as **sensitive personal
information**. We apply privacy-protective defaults (see Section 3) and
never expose it to other users unless you explicitly opt in.

## 1. What we collect
- Account data: email, hashed password (via Supabase Auth), username
- Profile data: display name, avatar, country/region (if provided)
- Contribution data: which communities you've supported and how many
  points, tied to your account
- Payment data: we do not store card numbers; payment processing is
  handled by our payment provider, and we retain only transaction
  identifiers and amounts needed for accounting and dispute handling
- Technical data: standard server logs, error reports

## 2. What we never expose publicly
- Email address, phone number
- Payment details
- Precise location
- Private purchase history
- Private religious affiliation
- Internal moderation/fraud review data

## 3. Your privacy controls
Default settings hide your username, points, country, and profile from
public view. You can opt in per-setting in Settings → Privacy.
